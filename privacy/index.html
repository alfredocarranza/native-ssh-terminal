Native SSH Terminal Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Native SSH Terminal is published by Carranza Technologies LLC. We designed the app to connect your device directly to SSH servers you choose, without routing terminal traffic through a Carranza Technologies service.

Data we collect

Carranza Technologies does not collect personal data, terminal traffic, server addresses, usernames, passwords, private keys, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or telemetry through Native SSH Terminal.

Data stored on your device

Saved-host metadata and trusted host-key fingerprints are stored in the app container with iOS file protection. Passwords and Ed25519 private keys are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Retention and deletion

Saved-host metadata and its saved password remain until you delete that host. The default Ed25519 private key remains until you use Delete Key on the Keys tab. Trusted host keys are retained independently from saved hosts so the app can continue detecting unexpected key changes; use Settings > Clear All Trusted Host Keys to remove every trust record. Clearing trust records causes the next connection to each server to be treated as first use. Deleting the app removes its container, while Keychain retention after uninstallation is controlled by iOS.

Connections to servers you choose

The app transmits SSH authentication data, commands, and terminal traffic directly to servers you configure. Those servers may log or process data according to policies set by their owners. Carranza Technologies does not control user-selected servers.

Clipboard and local network

The app accesses the clipboard only when you copy, paste, or use terminal clipboard features. Local Network access is used only to connect to hosts you choose on your local network.

Children

The app is a general-purpose developer utility and does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes

If app capabilities or data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

Questions may be sent to alfredo@carranza.tech. Support email is processed by our email provider and retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer and document the request. You may request deletion of a support conversation. Please redact hostnames, usernames, IP addresses, terminal output, passwords, and private keys before sending email.
